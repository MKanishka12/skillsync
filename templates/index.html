The problem

Campus talent is scattered. Students form teams with the friends they already know, so strong coders never meet designers, and good project ideas stall because nobody knows who could help. Skills stay hidden inside departments and year groups.

Our solution

One place where every student lists their skills and interests, and every project lists what it needs. SkillSync does the matching with a transparent score you can read and trust, so finding a teammate takes minutes instead of weeks.

How it works

  1. 1

    Build your profile

    Register with your department, year, skills and interests. Update them any time as you learn.

  2. 2

    Get matched

    See students ranked by shared skills, shared interests and the new skills they bring to your team.

  3. 3

    Post or join projects

    Publish a project with the skills it needs, or browse the hub and express interest in one that fits you.
